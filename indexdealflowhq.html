How it works

You approve the list. I do the outreach. You take the calls.

  1. You approve a list

    Five to ten companies, the role to reach at each one, and the first message. Nothing goes out before you approve it in writing.

  2. I reach out

    First message and follow-ups to the person in that role, by email and DM. Replies that need you come straight to you.

  3. You take the call

    When the person joins and stays at least 5 minutes, the call counts as held and is billed.

Questions

No. I guarantee the process: approved list, real first messages, and a written count of every call.

Only the people in the roles you approve, at the companies you approve. I can suggest names, and you decide.

What you want partners for, the kind of company you want to meet, and a calendar link for the calls.
