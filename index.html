How it works

You tell me the clients you want. I find them. You decide.

  1. You set the profile

    Which corridors you serve, what monthly volume is worth your time, and the kind of company you want as a client.

  2. I make the introduction

    I email you and the client together. The client confirms in writing that they want the introduction.

  3. You serve the client

    You set the price and run the relationship. I get a share only while the client pays you fees.

Terms in plain words

What I promise and what I don't.

  • I tell every client that you may pay me if they become your client. I do not share the amount. You set the client's price.
  • I send a monthly fee report for each client, and a yearly written confirmation on request.
  • The terms stay in force if your company is sold or renamed.
  • I do not promise a number of clients, and I do not promise a deal.
  • Your company name is used only with your written approval.
  • I make no commitments on your behalf.

Questions

Before you write to me.

Do you guarantee clients?

No. I guarantee the process: introductions only to the profile you set, in writing, with a monthly report on every client I bring.

What do I need to give you?

The corridors you serve, the monthly volume that is worth your time, and who on your side takes the introduction.

Why a share and not a fixed fee?

I control the introduction. You control whether the client is worth having. A share means I only earn when you do.

Can the client know I get paid?

Yes, I tell them. They hear it from me, and the price they pay is still yours to set.
